Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the platform and its data

Who maintains the BARMM Labour Market Readiness platform?

The platform is maintained by the Bureau of Investments of the Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (MTIT–BOI), BARMM, as part of Phase II policy support on labour market readiness for investment.

Where do the dashboard figures come from?

Headline indicators come from official Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) releases for BARMM, including the 2025 Labour Force Survey and the 2024 Regional Accounts, together with TESDA, ILO and MTIT–BOI documents. Every source is listed under Data & Sources in the dashboard.

Can I request disaggregated or updated labour market data?

Yes. Use the form and choose “Data & statistics request”. Where we do not hold the data, we will point you to the PSA Regional Statistical Services Office or the relevant ministry. We cannot release microdata that is not already public.

Does a SIPP-mapped activity mean there are confirmed jobs or projects?

No. The 2026 SIPP Workforce Map shows indicative occupational pathways for preferred investment activities. It does not represent approved projects, job vacancies or confirmed skills shortages; these need project and employer evidence.

How quickly will I receive a reply?

We aim to acknowledge inquiries within three working days. Requests that need coordination with other agencies may take longer; we will tell you if that is the case.
