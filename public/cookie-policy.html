1. About this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the BARMM Labour Market Readiness platform (the “Platform”), operated by MTIT–BOI, BARMM, uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies, and how you control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

“Cookies” here includes HTTP cookies and similar technologies such as localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB and the service-worker cache.

2. How consent works

Only strictly necessary storage is active by default. After you have spent two minutes actively using the Platform, a Cookie Consent & Settings pop-up asks whether you also allow preference and analytics storage. You can accept all, reject non-essential storage, or choose each category. Until you choose, nothing non-essential is set. We ask again after twelve months or if our use of cookies changes materially.

3. Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the Platform to work and to remember your privacy choice. These cannot be switched off in our settings, but you can delete them in your browser.

Preferences

Remember how you use the dashboard, such as the last section you viewed. Off unless you opt in.

Analytics

Google Analytics helps us understand, in aggregate, which labour market evidence is most used so we can improve it. The analytics script is not loaded at all unless you opt in. We do not use advertising, retargeting or social-media tracking cookies.

5. Third-party requests

Loading web fonts (Google Fonts) and the charting library (jsDelivr) sends your IP address and browser details to those providers, as with any web resource. These requests do not set cookies on our behalf. See the Privacy Policy for details.

6. Managing your choices

You can change or withdraw consent at any time, on any page, using Cookie settings in the side panel or footer. When you withdraw analytics consent, we stop loading Google Analytics and delete its cookies for this site. When you withdraw preference consent, we delete the stored preference.

You can also manage storage in your browser: Chrome/Edge — Settings › Privacy and security › Cookies and other site data; Firefox — Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data; Safari — Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data. Clearing site data also deletes Project Register records stored on your device, so export them first.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this policy if our use of cookies changes. Questions can be sent through our contact page or to our Data Protection Officer at boi@bangsamoro.gov.ph.
