1. Our commitment

The Bureau of Investments of the Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (MTIT–BOI), BARMM, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the BARMM Labour Market Readiness platform (the “Platform”) processes, why, and the rights you have under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), its Implementing Rules and Regulations and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

In short: you can use the dashboard without creating an account or giving us personal information. Dashboard data is a public statistical snapshot. Your Project Register records stay in your own browser. We only receive personal data if you contact us, and we use analytics only if you opt in.

We do not ask for government-issued IDs, financial data, health data or other sensitive personal information. Please do not include such information in your messages.

4. Purposes and lawful basis

  • Responding to inquiries — to answer, route or follow up your message. Basis: your consent and our public mandate (Sections 12(a) and 12(e), Data Privacy Act).
  • Improving the Platform — aggregate usage statistics to understand which evidence is most used. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Security and continuity — protecting the Platform from abuse and keeping it available. Basis: legitimate interest and the performance of a public function.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or automated decisions that affect you.

5. Service providers and international transfers

We rely on a small number of service providers that may process limited data on our behalf, some outside the Philippines:

  • Netlify — website hosting, content delivery and contact-form storage;
  • Google Fonts — web typography (your IP address is visible to Google when fonts load);
  • jsDelivr — delivery of the open-source Chart.js charting library;
  • Google Analytics — usage measurement, only if you consent.

Where data is transferred abroad, we rely on the provider’s contractual and security commitments, consistent with Section 21 of the Data Privacy Act on accountability for transfers.

6. Retention

  • Contact inquiries: kept for up to two (2) years after the matter is closed, then deleted, unless a longer period is required by law or audit rules.
  • Analytics data: retained for a maximum of fourteen (14) months.
  • Consent records: kept in your browser for twelve (12) months, after which we ask again.
  • Browser-stored Project Register and preferences: kept until you delete them.

7. Security

The Platform is served only over HTTPS. Access to contact submissions is limited to authorised MTIT–BOI personnel. Reference data is read-only, and the Platform does not maintain user accounts or passwords. No system is perfectly secure; in the event of a personal data breach that meets the NPC notification threshold, we will notify the NPC and affected individuals as required by NPC Circular 16-03.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to rectification, to data portability, to file a complaint and to be indemnified for damages. To exercise these rights, write to our Data Protection Officer at boi@bangsamoro.gov.ph or use the contact form and choose “Privacy or data protection request”. We will respond within fifteen (15) working days. You can withdraw analytics or preference consent at any time via .

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph or complaints@privacy.gov.ph.

9. Children

The Platform is intended for policy-makers, practitioners, researchers and the general public. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18 through the contact form. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Platform or the law. The “Last updated” date shows the latest revision. Significant changes affecting consent will trigger a new cookie consent request.
