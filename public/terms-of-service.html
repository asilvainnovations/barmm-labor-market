1. Acceptance of these terms

These Terms of Service govern your use of the BARMM Labour Market Readiness platform at labor-market.mtit-barmm.com (the “Platform”), operated by the Bureau of Investments of the Ministry of Trade, Investments and Tourism (“MTIT–BOI”, “we”, “us”) of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM). By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to these Terms, our Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

2. Purpose of the Platform

The Platform is a public-interest workforce intelligence tool. It presents labour force indicators, employment structure, sector and investment signals, provincial profiles, TVET outcomes and an indicative 2026 Strategic Investment Priority Plan (SIPP) Workforce Map to support evidence-based policy, programme design and investment-responsive skills planning in BARMM.

The Platform is provided for information and analysis. It is not an official statistical release, an investment approval system, a job-matching service or a licensing or permit portal.

3. Data sources and interpretation

Figures are drawn from a versioned snapshot of the BARMM Labour Market Statistical Workbook (SIPP-Aligned), compiled from Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), TESDA, ILO and MTIT–BOI sources listed under Data & Sources. When using the Platform you agree to observe these analytical safeguards:

  • Official statistics prevail. Where a figure differs from the latest PSA release, the PSA release is authoritative.
  • SIPP is not demand. SIPP eligibility does not prove the existence of a BARMM project, investment approval, financing, jobs or a skills shortage. SIPP Workforce Map pathways are indicative and must be validated with project and employer evidence.
  • Leads are not investments. Investment-lead shares are early signals of market interest, not approved capital expenditure or expected employment.
  • Blanks are not zero. Missing source values are shown as blank or “—” and must not be read as zero.
  • Different reference years. GRDP (2024) and employment (2025) shares have different reference years and denominators; comparisons are descriptive, not productivity ratios.
  • TVET denominators. TVET graduate employment rates refer to graduates in the labour force for the stated cohort, not to all graduates or trainees.

4. Acceptable use

You may view, download, export and share content from the Platform for lawful, non-misleading purposes. You agree not to:

  • misrepresent Platform figures, remove source attributions or present indicative analysis as official statistics or government commitments;
  • attempt to disrupt, overload, probe or gain unauthorised access to the Platform or its hosting infrastructure, contrary to Republic Act No. 10175 (Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012);
  • use automated tools to scrape the Platform in a way that degrades service for other users;
  • submit unlawful, defamatory or malicious content through the contact form, or include another person’s personal data without a lawful basis.

5. Project Register and locally stored records

The Investment & Workforce Project Register lets you record project evidence in your own browser (IndexedDB, with a localStorage fallback). These records stay on your device, are not sent to MTIT–BOI, and are not synchronised across devices or users. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter, for exporting any records you wish to keep, and for not recording personal or confidential information you are not authorised to hold. Clearing browser data or using “Reset local project records” permanently deletes them.
